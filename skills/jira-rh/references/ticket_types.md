# List of ticket types and their statuses

This is a list of ticket types and the possible status they can have in order. For example:
- An active ticket means a ticket that is still not in "REVIEW", or "VERIFIED", or "CLOSED".
- A ticket finished ticket means that is in "REVIEW", or "VERIFIED", or "CLOSED".

Types:
- Epic: "NEW", "REFINEMENT", "BACKLOG", "IN PROGRESS", "REVIEW", "CLOSED".
- Bug: "NEW", "REFINEMENT", "PLANNING", "BACKLOG", "IN PROGRESS", "REVIEW", "VERIFIED", "CLOSED".
- Task: "BACKLOG", "TO DO", "IN PROGRESS", "CLOSED".
- Spike: "BACKLOG", "TO DO", "IN PROGRESS", "CLOSED".
- Feature Request: "NEW", "REFINEMENT", "IN PROGRESS", "CLOSED".
